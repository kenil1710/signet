Signet dashboard

Every website sealed with Signet on X Layer mainnet (chain 196), read live from the chain in your browser. No account; a wallet is needed only to approve, freeze or create a gate.

Projects

For each sealed domain: its gate, the current release and its risk tier, the three approvers, open proposals with their approvals and timelocks, observe or enforce mode, and whether the domain confirms its gate (/.well-known/signet.json). Approvers approve, cancel, freeze and publish here, in their own wallets. signetguard.xyz is Signet's own site.

Proof

The proof page needs no wallet. It re-runs release-policy circuit #6 and Guard circuit #7 on all 512 inputs against their reference functions, checks the circuit beacon has no owner, and recomputes the file, allowlist and origins hashes of every recent release from the files stored on X Layer.
